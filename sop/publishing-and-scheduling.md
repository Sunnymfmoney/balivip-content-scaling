# SOP: Publishing and scheduling

## Weekly rhythm

**Plan Monday, build Tuesday and Wednesday, schedule Thursday, post all week.**

Never build on the day you post. That is how brand rules get skipped.

## Building the calendar

1. Open `templates/content-calendar.csv`.
2. Fill one week. For each row: date, format, pillar, working title, ask,
   destination, asset source.
3. **Check the mix before you build anything.** Roughly 40 aesthetic, 40 authority,
   20 conversion. If you have four conversion posts in seven, cut two.
4. Check segment balance against `sop/sorting-by-wedding-type.md`.
5. Check that no two consecutive posts carry the same ask.

## Meta Business Suite

1. Planner, then Create post.
2. Carousel: upload in numeric order. **Check the order after upload**, it does
   reorder files sometimes.
3. Paste the caption from `CAPTIONS.md` or the deck folder. Check the first line
   survived, that is the ask.
4. Set the cover frame for reels manually. The auto-pick is usually the worst frame.
5. Schedule, do not post now. Posting now skips the review step.

**Known limits:** Business Suite cannot schedule a story link sticker, and its
carousel preview crops differently from the app. Check on a phone before scheduling.

## Timing

Audiences are Australia, Singapore, India, the UK. There is no single good hour.

Starting points, Bali time:
- **Reels** 11:00 or 19:00
- **Carousels** 19:00 to 21:00, when people read rather than scroll
- **Stories** through the day, at least one before 10:00 and one after 19:00

Change one variable at a time and give it two weeks before deciding anything.

## Cadence

| Format | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Reels | 3 a week |
| Carousels | 2 a week |
| Stories | Daily, 5 to 7 frames |
| Proof post | 1 a week |
| Pinned posts | Change rarely |

More than this is not better. Consistency at this level beats bursts.

## Pinned posts: all at once or spaced

**Post all three within an hour, then pin all three.**

The grid orders by post time, most recent leftmost, so **post pin 3 first and pin 1
last.** Then open the profile on a phone and confirm the order. Spacing them over
days leaves the profile half-built in the meantime, which is worse than waiting.

## Testing a new angle

One new angle at a time, three posts, two weeks. Judge on **saves and DMs**, not
reach. A post with modest reach and six DMs beat a viral one with none.

Never test a new angle on a pinned post.
