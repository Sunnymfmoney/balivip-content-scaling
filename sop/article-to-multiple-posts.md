# SOP: One article feature to six posts

## 1. Capture it properly

- Save the article as a PDF and put the link somewhere permanent.
- Screenshot the masthead and the headline clean, with no browser UI.
- Pull every quote that mentions BaliVIP, verbatim, into a note.

## 2. Find the three assets inside it

1. **The strongest single sentence.** Usually one line does all the work.
2. **The claim.** What the piece says BaliVIP is good at.
3. **The subject.** The wedding, venue or trend it covered.

## 3. The six posts

| # | Post | Format | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The announcement. Masthead, one pull quote | Single image | Link in bio |
| 2 | The pull quotes, expanded | Carousel | Link in bio |
| 3 | The strongest claim, argued | Reel | Comment keyword |
| 4 | Story set with the link sticker | Stories | Link sticker |
| 5 | Saved to a Press highlight | Highlight | Permanent |
| 6 | The wedding or work it covered | Reel or carousel | None |

Post 4 matters more than it looks. The story link sticker is where the link actually
gets tapped. The feed post builds credibility, stories drive the clicks.

## 4. Rules

- Never distort or recolour a masthead.
- Quote the article verbatim. Do not paraphrase into a stronger claim.
- Do not imply an endorsement the piece did not make.
- Link to the article, not the publication homepage.
- Space these across two weeks. Six press posts in six days reads as insecure.
