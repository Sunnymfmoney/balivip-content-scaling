# SOP: Sorting content by wedding type

## Why this exists

The account speaks to four different fears. Content sorted by wedding type is how you
make sure you are not posting six weeks of the same segment because that folder was
easiest to reach.

## The codes

`photos/manifest.tsv` prefixes:

| Prefix | Type |
|---|---|
| `INI` | Indian and South Asian |
| `INT` | International and mixed |
| `WES` | International, Western-presenting couples |
| `LOC` | Local, detail, and Bali context |
| `VEN` | Venue and setting |
| `PRF` | Reserved for review slides |

`make list-photos` shows everything registered.

## The balance check

Once a month, count what actually went out by segment.

| Segment | Target share of feed |
|---|---|
| Indian and South Asian | 30 percent |
| International and mixed | 35 percent |
| Chinese and East Asian | 15 percent |
| Australian and Western | 20 percent |

These are starting numbers, not law. Adjust to where the enquiries actually come from
once `analytics/` has three months in it.

## The known gap

The library is thin on Indian photographs relative to how much Indian content the
account needs. **More Indian source material is worth more than any further editing.**
Flag this to the team every time you shoot an Indian wedding.

Chinese and East Asian material is thinner still. Content for that segment currently
has to lean on written slides rather than photographs, which is a real weakness.

## When tagging a new folder

Tag by **what the wedding needed**, not by what the couple looked like. A mixed couple
running a tea ceremony is Chinese-segment content, because that is the fear it speaks
to.
