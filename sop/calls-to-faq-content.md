# SOP: Planning calls to FAQ content

The best education content in this account comes from questions couples actually
asked. Not invented questions. Real ones.

## 1. Capture

After every planning or discovery call, write down:

- Every question the couple asked
- Anything they were visibly relieved to hear
- Anything they were surprised by
- Anything they got wrong about how it works

Ten seconds of notes per call. That is the whole input.

## 2. Find the repeats

**A question asked twice is a post. A question asked five times is a pinned post.**

Keep a running list. Tally it. The tally is your content calendar and it is more
reliable than any trend.

## 3. Turn a question into a post

A question is not a post. The shape is:

1. **The question, as they asked it.** Their words, not tidied up.
2. **The honest answer**, including the part that is inconvenient.
3. **The thing they had not considered.** This is the actual value.
4. **The ask**, if it fits.

## 4. Formats

| Signal | Format |
|---|---|
| Asked twice | One story frame |
| Asked five times | Reel or carousel |
| Asked on every call | Pinned post or lead magnet |
| Relief when answered | Testimonial-adjacent post |
| A common wrong assumption | A "do not" post, the highest performing shape |

## 5. Using a transcript

If the call was recorded and you have a transcript, use
`prompts/content-from-transcript.md`. Give Claude the transcript and it returns the
questions, the surprises and the misconceptions, sorted by how often they came up.

**Never publish anything a client said** without asking. Use the pattern, not the
person. "Couples often ask" is fine. "One of our couples said" needs permission.
