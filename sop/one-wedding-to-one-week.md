# SOP: One wedding to one week of content

One good wedding folder is a full week. This is the highest leverage workflow here.

## Before anything

**Consent, in writing.** Feed, stories, ads, or all three. No exceptions.

## The week

| Day | Post | Format | Pillar | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | The recap | Reel | Aesthetic | None |
| Tue | What this culture's events needed | Carousel | Cultural | Tell us your ceremonies |
| Wed | Setup to finished | Reel | Authority | None |
| Thu | The review from this couple | Carousel or story | Proof | Link in bio |
| Fri | One detail done properly | Reel | Authority | Comment keyword |
| Sat | Guest reactions or blue hour | Reel | Aesthetic | None |
| Sun | The lesson this wedding taught | Carousel | Education | Comment keyword |

Stories run daily alongside, from the same folder. Two interactive frames minimum.

**Three asks in seven posts.** That is the right ratio. More and the account starts
to feel like a shop.

## Extracting the week

1. Sort the folder into the four piles (`drive-folder-to-10-reels.md`, step 2).
2. Find the wedding's **one lesson**. What did this wedding need that a couple would
   not have guessed? That is Sunday's carousel and it is the most valuable post.
3. Find the **one detail**. Something small done properly.
4. Find the **one save**. Something that went wrong and was fixed. If nothing did,
   skip it, do not invent one.
5. Check whether this couple left a review. If they did, that is Thursday.

## Do not

- Do not use the same photograph twice in the week.
- Do not put an ask on every post.
- Do not name the venue without clearance.
- Do not invent a guest count, a budget, or a number of events.
