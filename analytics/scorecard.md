# Post scorecard

## How to use it

Log every post in `analytics/post-log.csv` on the day it goes out. Fill the numbers
**7 days later**, not before. Instagram redistributes for about a week and early
numbers make you cancel things that were working.

## What to record

| Field | Where from | Why |
|---|---|---|
| date, title, format | You | Identity |
| pillar | You | Which of the eight |
| type | You | aesthetic, authority, or conversion |
| segment | You | Indian, international, Chinese, western, all |
| ask | You | The single CTA |
| destination | You | keyword, DM, link, WhatsApp, form, none |
| reach | Insights | Distribution |
| saves | Insights | **The best single signal** |
| shares | Insights | Reach multiplier |
| comments | Insights | Real engagement, plus keyword triggers |
| dms | ManyChat or inbox | Intent |
| link_clicks | Link tool | Intent |
| enquiries | CRM | The only number that pays |
| notes | You | What you would change |

## How to read it

**Saves are the strongest predictor of an enquiry.** Someone saving a post about what
their culture's wedding needs is planning. Someone liking it is scrolling.

Rules of thumb:

| Pattern | Read |
|---|---|
| High reach, low saves | Pretty, forgettable. Fine for aesthetic, failed for authority |
| Low reach, high saves | The content is right, the hook is not. Rewrite frame one and repost in six weeks |
| High saves, no DMs | No ask, or the wrong ask. The value was there and the door was shut |
| High comments, no DMs | Keyword automation is broken. Check `automations/troubleshooting.md` |
| High DMs, no enquiries | Nobody is qualifying, or the DM script is off |
| Good everything, no leads | The destination is broken. Test it end to end today |

## Monthly review

Answer five questions:

1. What was the actual mix, aesthetic to authority to conversion? Target 40/40/20.
2. Which single post produced the most enquiries? Make three more like it.
3. Which pillar produced the fewest saves? Cut or fix it.
4. Which segment did we underserve?
5. What did we promise in a caption that the destination did not deliver?

Question 5 is the one that costs money and the one people skip.

## What not to do

- Do not judge a post before 7 days.
- Do not chase reach. An enquiry is worth more than 100,000 views.
- Do not change three things at once. You will learn nothing.
- Do not delete an underperformer. Log it. The pattern is the asset.
