# Prompt: A week from one wedding

## Inputs needed
- The wedding folder
- The wedding type and what stood out
- Whether consent is confirmed
- Any review from this couple

## The prompt

```
Plan a week of BaliVIP content from this wedding.

Folder:   [path]
Type:     [Indian / international / Chinese / western / mixed]
Stood out:[what was notable: a ritual, a save, a detail, the scale]
Consent:  [confirmed for feed / stories / ads / not yet]
Review:   [paste it, or "none"]

If consent is not confirmed, say so and stop.

Give me seven days: format, pillar, type, working title, ask, destination, and which
assets each needs.

Rules:
- Three asks maximum across seven posts
- No photograph used twice in the week
- Find the wedding's one lesson, that is the strongest post of the week
- Find the one detail and the one save, if there was one. Do not invent a save

Read sop/one-wedding-to-one-week.md first.

Then fill templates/content-calendar.csv and build Monday's post.
```

## Output format
The seven day table, the calendar rows, and the first post built.

## Quality standard
A real week someone can execute, matched to assets that actually exist in the folder.

## Brand rules
Consent before anything. No venue named without clearance. No invented numbers.

## CTA rules
Three asks in seven posts. Not one per post.
