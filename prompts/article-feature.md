# Prompt: Article feature

## Inputs needed
- The article link, or the PDF
- The publication and date
- A clean masthead image

## The prompt

```
Turn this press feature into a set of BaliVIP posts.

Article:     [link or pasted text]
Publication: [name]
Date:        [month year]

Give me:
1. Every quote that mentions BaliVIP, verbatim
2. The single strongest sentence in the piece
3. The claim the article makes about BaliVIP
4. Six posts: announcement, pull-quote carousel, reel on the strongest claim,
   story set with link sticker, highlight, and a post on the work it covered
5. Captions for each

Read playbooks/article-features.md and sop/article-to-multiple-posts.md first.

Quote verbatim. Do not paraphrase into a stronger claim than the article made.
```

## Output format
Quotes, the six posts, captions, and a suggested two-week schedule.

## Quality standard
Verbatim quotes. No implied endorsement. Link to the article, not the homepage.

## Brand rules
Never distort a masthead. Never imply a feature that did not happen. Space the six
posts over two weeks.

## CTA rules
Link in bio on the announcement, story link sticker for actual clicks.
