# Prompt: Caption

## Inputs needed
- What the post shows or teaches
- The ask and its destination
- The format

## The prompt

```
Write the caption for this BaliVIP post.

Post shows:  [what is on screen]
Teaches:     [the one idea, or "nothing, it is aesthetic"]
Ask:         [the CTA, or "none"]
Goes to:     [destination]
Format:      [reel / carousel / single image]

Structure:
  Line 1: the ask, with the deliverable and the timing
  Then:   the hook restated
  Then:   the body, one short paragraph per idea
  Last:   the ask again, two or three words

If the post is aesthetic with no ask, skip the ask lines and write three
sentences that let the photograph do the work.

Read strategy/03-cta-and-conversion-rules.md first.
```

## Output format
The caption, ready to paste. No hashtag block unless asked.

## Quality standard
- The ask is the first line. Most readers never tap "more"
- Short paragraphs. One idea each
- Third grade reading level
- No em dashes

## Brand rules
No invented numbers. No begging, no urgency, no exclamation mark in the ask.
American spelling.
