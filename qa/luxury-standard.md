# The luxury standard

What separates this account from a wedding vendor account. Read once, then it should
be instinct.

## Restraint

Luxury is what you leave out.

- One idea per frame. Not two.
- One ask per post. Not two.
- Fewer slides, not more. Ten is a ceiling, not a target.
- No emoji in headlines. Sparingly in captions, if at all.
- No stickers, no arrows, no "swipe →" graphics beyond the built-in one.
- No exclamation marks in an ask.

## Pace

Calm reads as expensive. Frantic reads as cheap.

- Slow pushes on photographs, never a snap zoom.
- Hold a wide shot. Let the room breathe.
- No cut every four frames. That is a different brand.
- Ambient or instrumental audio by default. A trending sound has to earn its place.

## Type

- Cormorant Garamond for headlines. Jost for everything else. No substitutes.
- Never stretch, outline, or drop-shadow the type.
- Copy sits in the quiet part of the frame, always with a scrim.
- Never on a face.

## Colour

- Consistent grade across a set. One warm post next to one cold post looks accidental.
- Never a filter that shifts skin tone.
- The palette is ink, cream, warm white and gold. Nothing else.

## Photographs

- Real work only. No stock, ever.
- A 10 out of 10 or it does not go in. Alignment, light, a genuine expression.
  A real smile shows in the eyes.
- Setup and behind the scenes are allowed to be rough. That is honesty, not sloppiness.
- Never reuse a photograph inside a set.

## Language

- Specific beats grand. "A driveway and room to turn around" beats "flawless
  execution".
- Never superlatives about yourself.
- Never desperate. No "we would love to", no "don't miss out".
- Third grade reading level, and still elegant. Short words are not cheap words.

## The test

Put the post next to the work itself. If the post looks like it was made by a
different, smaller company than the one that produced the wedding, it is not ready.
