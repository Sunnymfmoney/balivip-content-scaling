# ManyChat setup

## Account

ManyChat logs in with the Instagram account, not a personal email. Use the social
media team address so nobody has to chase an OTP from someone who is not in the room.

Connect via Meta, log in as the BaliVIP Wedding Instagram, grant every permission it
asks for. Missing permissions is the most common reason a keyword silently fails.

## The one rule about keywords

**Short. Three to five letters. One word.**

Every extra letter loses people, and every space loses more. Two-word triggers fail
often enough that they are not worth it.

## Current status

The trigger word is **not locked**. Candidates:

| Word | Letters | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **VIP** | 3 | Shortest. Fits the brand language: VIP couple, VIP destination |
| **BALI** | 4 | Also passes. Used in some older caption drafts |
| BALI VIP | 8, two words | Fails the rule. Do not use |

Pick one and use it everywhere. A different keyword per post is how you end up with
five half-working automations.

## Building a flow

1. **Trigger.** Comment on a specific post, or on any post, containing the keyword.
2. **Public reply.** One line, warm, no link. "Sent it to your DMs."
   Instagram limits how many identical public replies you can post, so write three
   variations and rotate them.
3. **DM, message 1.** Acknowledge and deliver, or ask the one question you need.
4. **DM, message 2.** The deliverable, or the link.
5. **Tag.** Tag the contact with the post name so you can trace which post produced
   which enquiry.
6. **Handoff.** Route to a human inbox. Never leave a thread dead-ended in a bot.

## The rule that keeps this from cheapening the brand

**The DM has to sound like a person.** No "Hey there! 👋 Thanks for your interest!!"

Write it the way the team actually writes:

> Hi, thanks for commenting. Here are the real cost ranges for a Bali wedding at a
> few different guest counts. If you tell me roughly how many people you are
> expecting, I can narrow it down properly.

One question, one deliverable, no pressure.

## Somebody has to watch the inbox

An automation that opens a conversation nobody answers is worse than no automation.
Name one person whose job is to reply, qualify, and book. Their job is not to close.
