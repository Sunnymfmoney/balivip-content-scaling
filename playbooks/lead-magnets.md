# Playbook: Lead magnet content

**Goal** An enquiry, or at minimum a name and a way to reach them.

**When to use** Roughly one post in five. This is the conversion 20 percent.

## What a good lead magnet is here

It answers the thing they are actually afraid of, and it is genuinely useful without
a call. Anything that requires a sales conversation to be useful is not a lead magnet.

Working ideas, best first:

1. **Real cost ranges at your guest count.** The single strongest, because price
   opacity is the biggest fear and nobody else publishes it.
2. **The planning guide.** What happens, in order, from first message to last song.
3. **The venue questions checklist.** What to ask before booking anything.
4. **The wet weather plan.** What a real contingency looks like.
5. **The cultural events checklist.** How many spaces your events actually need.

## Structure

1. **Name the fear.** One line.
2. **Name the deliverable.** Specific. "Cost ranges at your guest count", not "a guide".
3. **Name the speed.** "The same day."
4. **Remove the objection.** "No form, no call."
5. **One ask.**

## Caption template

```
[Ask + deliverable + speed + removed objection, in one line]

[The fear, named]

[Two or three lines on what is actually inside it]

[Ask again, two or three words]
```

## CTA options

**Comment keyword** is the best performing, because comments feed reach and there is
no friction. Link in bio second. Never an enquiry form for a cold audience.

## The destination has to exist

This is where lead magnet posts fail. Before publishing:

- The keyword automation is live and tested from a second account.
- The thing being promised actually exists and is finished.
- The link works on mobile.
- Someone is watching the inbox.

See `qa/pre-publish-checklist.md` and `automations/troubleshooting.md`.

## Common mistakes

- Promoting a magnet that does not exist yet.
- "DM for more info." Name the deliverable.
- Gating something worthless behind a form.
- Promising "no form" and then linking to a form. This has already happened. Pin 1
  says "no form, no call", so its keyword must be answered in the DM, not by a link.
- Running lead magnet posts back to back. Space them.

## Examples

1. **Comment VIP and we send you the real cost ranges for a Bali wedding at your
   guest count, the same day. No form, no call.**
2. **The five questions to ask any venue.** Carousel teaching three of them, keyword
   for all five.
3. **Reply RAIN and we send you our wet weather plan.** Story set.
4. **The planning guide.** Link in bio, from a process carousel.
5. **Tell us your ceremonies and we come back the same day with how many spaces you
   need.** Not a document, a human answer. Often the strongest of all.
