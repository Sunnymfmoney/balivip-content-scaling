# Playbook: Article and press features

**Goal** Borrowed authority. A third party saying it is worth more than the brand
saying it.

**When to use** Every time a feature lands. One feature is worth a week of content.

## The rule

**Lead with the publication, not the brand.** "Featured in [publication]" is the
whole asset. Do not bury it.

Never fabricate or imply a feature that did not happen. Never use a masthead you do
not have permission to use.

## What one feature turns into

See `sop/article-to-multiple-posts.md` for the full workflow. Short version, one
article becomes:

1. **The announcement.** Single image, the masthead, one pull quote. Link in bio.
2. **The pull quote carousel.** Three to five quotes from the piece, expanded.
3. **A reel** built on the strongest claim in the article.
4. **A story set** with the link sticker, which is the only place the link actually
   gets tapped.
5. **A highlight** so it lives on the profile permanently.
6. **The behind the scenes.** The wedding or work the article covered.

## Structure for the announcement post

1. The masthead or the article header.
2. One pull quote, verbatim.
3. One line of context: what the piece was about.
4. Link in bio.

## Hook templates

- We were featured in [publication].
- [Publication] asked us [question].
- "[Pull quote]" [Publication], [month year]
- The piece [publication] ran on [subject].

## CTA options

Link in bio (primary) · story link sticker · none if the point is credibility only.

## Visual direction

Clean and quiet. The publication's name does the work. Do not over-design a press
post, and never distort a masthead or place it on a busy photograph.

## Common mistakes

- Posting the feature once and never again. It has at least six posts in it.
- No link, or a link that goes to a homepage instead of the article.
- A screenshot with browser UI in it. Rebuild it clean.
- Implying an endorsement the article did not make.

## Examples

1. Masthead, one pull quote, link in bio.
2. Five pull quotes as a carousel, each expanded with a line of BaliVIP's own.
3. A reel on the single strongest claim from the piece.
4. Story set with link sticker, saved to a "Press" highlight.
5. The wedding the article covered, as a recap, captioned with the feature.
