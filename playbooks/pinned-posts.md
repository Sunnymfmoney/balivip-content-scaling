# Playbook: Pinned posts

**Goal** Convert profile visitors. These are the silent sales team. Every person who
taps the profile sees these three first.

**When to use** Rarely. Change them when the offer or the proof changes, not weekly.

## The three jobs

Three pinned posts, three different jobs, left to right.

| Pin | Job | Current |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Filter and hook.** Negative hook, earns the swipe, filters low budget | How NOT to Plan a Destination Wedding |
| 2 | **Prove range.** Cultural competence, the thing competitors cannot fake | All Cultures, All Races |
| 3 | **Remove fear of process.** What actually happens, in order | Our Planning Process |

Pin 1 is the widest net. Pin 3 is for someone already close to enquiring. Someone who
reads all three in order should have nothing left to be afraid of.

## Rules specific to pinned posts

- **Every pinned post carries proof at slide 2.** A real Google review with that
  couple's own photograph.
- **Every pinned post ends in one ask,** and the three asks are different.
- **The covers must look like a set** in the grid but not identical. Check
  `pinned-row-mobile.png` before publishing, not after.
- **No photograph repeats across the three.** Not one.
- Pin 1 may carry a comment trigger. Pins 2 and 3 use link in bio.

## Publishing

Post all three, then pin all three, then check the grid on a phone. The order in the
grid is by post time, most recent leftmost, so **post pin 3 first and pin 1 last.**

## Common mistakes

- Three decks that argue the same thing.
- A cover that only reads at full size. Check it at grid tile size.
- Changing them constantly. These are foundations, not campaign posts.
- Leaving a superseded version live because nobody checked which was current.
