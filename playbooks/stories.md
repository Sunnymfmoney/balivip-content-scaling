# Playbook: Stories

**Goal** Depth with people who already follow. Stories are where DMs come from.

**When to use** Daily. Five to seven frames a day is plenty.

**Build with** `make new NAME=x KIND=story`, then `make x`.

## Structure

Three to five frames. Never more than seven in one set.

1. **Stop.** One line, one image.
2. **Teach or show.** One idea.
3. **Ask.** One thing. A poll, a question box, a reply prompt, or a link.

## Safe zones

The build handles this, but if you make stories anywhere else: keep copy out of the
top 250px and the bottom 250px. Instagram puts its own UI there.

## Interaction beats broadcast

A story that asks something gets replies, and replies open a DM thread, which is
where enquiries actually happen. Use them:

- **Question box.** "What is the one thing stressing you out about your wedding?"
- **Poll.** "Beach or cliff?" "One day or three?"
- **Quiz.** "How many separate spaces does an Indian wedding need?"
- **Slider.** "How far along is your planning?"
- **Reply prompt.** "Reply with your date and we will tell you what is available."

**Two or three interactive frames a week produce more enquiries than any single post.**

## Hook templates

- Behind the scenes of [today]
- The bit nobody sees
- We got asked this twice today
- [N] hours until the ceremony
- Reply with your date

## CTA options

Story reply (best) · poll or question box · link sticker · DM prompt.

## Visual direction

Vertical, real, immediate. Stories are the one place slightly rougher footage is
correct. Over-polished stories read as ads and get skipped.

## Common mistakes

- Fifteen frames. Nobody finishes.
- A link sticker on every frame.
- Reposting the feed post with no change. Give story a reason to exist.
- Asking a question and never answering the replies. That is worse than not asking.

## Examples

1. **Setup timelapse, three frames, no ask.** Pure aesthetic.
2. **"What is stressing you out?" question box.** Then answer the best three as
   frames the next day. This is the highest-DM format in the whole system.
3. **Poll: beach or cliff.** Then a frame explaining what each actually requires.
4. **The rain plan.** Three frames, ends with "reply RAIN and we send you ours".
5. **A review, one frame, with the couple's photograph.** Ends with link to reviews.
