# Playbook: Carousels

**Goal** Saves and depth. Carousels are where BaliVIP proves competence.

**When to use** Two a week. Best format for teaching and for conversion.

**Build with** `make new NAME=x KIND=carousel`, then `make x`.

## Structure

1. **Cover.** One idea. The only slide most people see.
2. **Review.** A real Google review with that couple's own photograph.
3. **Body.** 6 to 10 slides. One idea each. No slide repeats another.
4. **Checklist** (optional). Lands the deck somewhere calm after a hard argument.
5. **CTA.** One ask.

Proof arrives before the pitch, at slide 2. That ordering is deliberate: it buys
permission for everything after it.

## The standalone rule

**Every headline must work alone.** Screenshot any slide, send it to one person with
no context. If it does not make sense, rewrite it. This is the single most useful
test in the system.

## Hook templates

- How NOT to plan a [thing]
- [N] things nobody tells you about [thing]
- What your [culture] wedding actually needs
- Our [process], from first message to last song
- All cultures, all races
- The questions to ask before you book anything
- What we do when it rains

## Caption template

Same as reels. Ask first, hook second, body, ask again.

## CTA options

Comment keyword · link in bio · WhatsApp · tell us your [X].

## Visual direction

- 1080x1350, always. The build enforces it.
- Cormorant Garamond for headlines, Jost for body. Never substitute.
- One photograph per slide, never repeated inside a set.
- Copy sits in the dark third. If the photograph is bright there, use a different one.
- No copy on a face.

## Common mistakes

- A headline that only makes sense after the previous slide.
- Two ideas on one slide.
- Fourteen slides. Nobody finishes. Ten is the practical ceiling.
- A CTA that does not answer the deck.
- Trimming a review so it fits. Never. The type size steps down for you.

## Examples

1. **How NOT to Plan a Destination Wedding.** Nine warnings, a five item checklist,
   one ask. Negative hook, filters low budget, earns the swipe.
2. **All Cultures, All Races.** Indian, Chinese, international, what each physically
   needs. Naresh Ahuja review at slide 2.
3. **Our Planning Process.** The real chronological workflow. Piyumi review at 2.
4. **What we do when it rains.** Wet weather plans, the tent story, the recovery.
5. **The five questions to ask any Bali planner.** Each one BaliVIP answers well.
