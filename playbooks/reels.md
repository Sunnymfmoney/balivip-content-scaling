# Playbook: Reels

**Goal** Reach. Reels are the only format that reliably puts BaliVIP in front of
people who do not follow it.

**When to use** Three a week. One aesthetic, one authority, one either.

## Structure

| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0.0 to 1.5s | Stop the scroll. Motion, not a portrait |
| Turn | 1.5 to 3s | Name the problem or the promise |
| Body | 3 to 15s | One idea. Two at most |
| Land | 15 to 22s | The payoff or the reveal |
| Ask | last 3s | One CTA, on screen and in the caption |

Under 25 seconds unless the footage genuinely carries longer. A recap can run 40.

## Hook rules

- **Motion in frame one.** Crowd energy, a baraat, confetti, a dress moving, fireworks.
- **Never open on a static seated portrait**, founder included.
- Text hook on screen within the first second. Most watch on mute.
- Say the thing. "Do not choose the venue first" beats "3 tips for your Bali wedding".

## Hook templates

- Do not [common mistake] for your Bali wedding.
- [Number] things nobody tells you about a destination wedding.
- Your [ritual] needs more space than you think.
- This is what [X] guests in Bali actually looks like.
- We planned this in [timeframe]. Here is what it took.
- The question nobody asks their wedding planner.
- If your venue cannot do this, walk away.
- What your [Indian / Chinese / international] wedding actually needs in Bali.

## Caption template

```
[Ask, one line, with deliverable and timing]

[The hook, restated]

[Two or three short paragraphs. One idea each]

[Ask, two or three words]
```

## CTA options

Comment keyword (best for reach) · story reply · link in bio · WhatsApp · none.

## Visual direction

- Calm and slow. Luxury does not cut every 4 frames.
- Real footage over stock, always.
- Subtitles on. Brand font, bottom third, never over a face.
- Colour graded consistently. No filters that shift skin tone.
- No trending audio that fights the mood. Instrumental or ambient by default.

## Common mistakes

- Opening on a logo or a title card. You have one second.
- Three ideas in twenty seconds. Nobody keeps any of them.
- Text over the bright part of the frame with no scrim.
- A CTA that does not match what the reel taught.
- Trending audio that makes a wedding look like a sale.

## Examples

1. **Do not choose the venue first.** Fast cuts of four different event spaces, text
   hook at 0s, one line of teaching, ask: comment VIP for cost ranges.
2. **A baraat needs a driveway.** Footage of the groom's entrance, then a cliff path.
   The contrast is the whole post. Ask: tell us your ceremonies.
3. **More staff than guests.** Behind the scenes of setup, timelapse, then the room
   finished. Ask: none. This is aesthetic and authority, no sell.
4. **The tent came down five hours before.** The Jasmine Tan review as voiceover over
   setup footage. Ask: link in bio to reviews.
5. **What 200 guests in Bali actually looks like.** One wide crane shot held long.
   Pure aesthetic. No ask.
