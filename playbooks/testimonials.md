# Playbook: Testimonials and reviews

**Goal** Break the fear with someone else's voice. The strongest conversion asset the
brand owns.

**When to use** Weekly. Every set of pinned posts carries one at slide 2.

## The absolute rules

1. **Verbatim or not at all.** Never trim a review to fit a card. Type steps down.
2. **The photograph must be that reviewer's own wedding.** No exceptions.
3. **Ask before it goes on a paid ad.** Public is not the same as consented.
4. Approved reviews live in `reference/reviews.md`. Copy from there.

## Structure

**Carousel or story frame:** photograph of that couple's wedding, review card over
the lower third, name, five stars, Google mark, and the credential strip underneath.

**Reel:** the review as on-screen text or voiceover, over footage of that wedding.

## Which review for which job

| The fear | Use |
|---|---|
| I have never been to Bali | Avalon & Rosata, Daniel, Joanne Rubelli |
| We are too far away | Ali & Fatima, Naresh Ahuja |
| Will they understand our culture | **Naresh Ahuja.** The strongest in the corpus |
| Will there be enough people | Denis Vodišek, Jayden Tucker |
| What if something goes wrong | **Jasmine Tan.** The tent story |
| Will I actually enjoy my own day | Mega Septiandara, James Lo, Avalon & Rosata |
| Is it worth it | k goody |

## Caption template

```
[The ask]

[One line framing what this couple was afraid of]

"[The review, verbatim]"
[Name]

[What you did that produced it, one or two lines]

[The ask]
```

## Common mistakes

- Trimming. It has already happened once and it removed the names of the people
  being praised.
- A stock photograph behind a real review.
- Five reviews in one carousel. One review, told properly, beats five skimmed.
- A screenshot of Google. Rebuild it in brand. Screenshots look cheap and leak UI.

## Examples

1. **Naresh Ahuja, full review, on the culture carousel.** He says a competitor knew
   the customs better and chose BaliVIP anyway. Do not cut that part. It is the proof.
2. **Jasmine Tan, the tent.** Reel. Setup footage, the story as text, the finished
   room. Ends with no ask. Pure authority.
3. **Mega Septiandara, one line, one frame story.** "Fully present." Link to reviews.
4. **Guest reactions.** "It feels like we should have bought tickets to this wedding."
   Aesthetic post, no ask.
5. **k goody, no unexpected charges.** Pairs with any pricing or budget content.
