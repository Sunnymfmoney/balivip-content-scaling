# Playbook: Venue and destination content

**Goal** Aesthetic reach plus authority. Show range without making promises.

**When to use** Weekly, usually as a reel or a story set.

## The hard rule

**Never name a venue in copy without clearance for that specific post.** Venue
relationships are commercial and they change. Never rank venues. Never say "the best
venue in Bali". You work with all of them.

Show the place. Teach what that *kind* of place requires. Do not name it unless
someone has cleared it.

## Structure

1. The place, beautifully. Wide, held long.
2. What this kind of setting physically requires.
3. The question a couple should ask about it.

## What to teach, by setting

| Setting | What it actually requires |
|---|---|
| Clifftop | Access for the groom's car, wind on the ceremony, generator, guest buggies |
| Beach | Tides and the ceremony time, sand and heels, sunset direction, permits |
| Chapel | Capacity is fixed, ceremony only, where the reception then goes |
| Villa estate | Exclusive use that day, curfew, neighbours, kitchen prep space |
| Garden / lawn | Wet weather plan, a real indoor room for a tea ceremony, lighting |
| Ballroom | The route in, the load-in time, whether it feels like Bali at all |

## Hook templates

- What a [clifftop] wedding actually needs.
- Before you fall in love with this view, ask this.
- The thing nobody checks about a [beach] ceremony.
- Two venues can look the same and run completely differently.
- Where does the groom's car turn around.

## CTA options

Tell us your ceremonies · comment keyword · none for pure aesthetic.

## Visual direction

Wide shots held long. Let the place breathe. No fast cutting on a landscape.
Golden hour and blue hour over midday. Never a real estate walkthrough.

## Common mistakes

- Naming a venue without clearance.
- A ranking or a "top 5".
- Implying exclusivity or a partnership that does not exist.
- A view with no lesson attached. Fine occasionally as pure aesthetic, weak as a habit.

## Examples

1. **Clifftop, one wide shot, one line:** "Some of Bali's best venues are reached
   single file down a cliff path. Ask how the groom arrives."
2. **Beach at two different tides.** Same spot, two hours apart. The lesson writes
   itself.
3. **A lawn is not a room.** Garden footage, then an indoor tea ceremony.
4. **Wet weather plan.** The same venue in rain and in sun.
5. **Blue hour reception, no copy, no ask.** Pure aesthetic.
