# Content pillars

## Three jobs, not one

Every post does exactly one of these. Deciding which one before you write is the
single highest leverage decision in the whole system.

| | **Aesthetic** | **Authority** | **Conversion** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job | Stop the scroll, prove taste | Prove competence | Produce an enquiry |
| Feels like | A beautiful moment | A useful lesson | An open door |
| CTA | None, or a soft follow | Soft, save or share | One hard, specific ask |
| Success | Reach, shares | Saves, follows | DMs, comments, link clicks |
| Risk if overused | Pretty and forgettable, no enquiries | Educational but never asks | Salesy, cheapens the brand |

**The mix that works: roughly 40 aesthetic, 40 authority, 20 conversion.**

Conversion posts only convert because the other 80 percent earned the right to ask.
Run conversion above 30 percent for two weeks and reach falls, because people scroll
past an account that always wants something.

## The pillars

**1. Wedding aesthetics** *(aesthetic)*
The work, beautifully. Decor, light, the couple, the room. No lesson, no ask.

**2. Real weddings and recaps** *(aesthetic and authority)*
One wedding told properly. What the couple wanted, what it took, what it looked like.

**3. Planning education** *(authority)*
The single strongest pillar for enquiries. Teach the thing they have not thought of.
Every "do not" post lives here.

**4. Cultural competence** *(authority)*
Indian, Chinese, international, mixed. Named rituals and what each one physically
needs. This is the pillar competitors cannot copy without the experience.

**5. Venue and destination** *(aesthetic and authority)*
What a place actually requires. Access, curfew, weather, capacity. Never a ranking,
never a named recommendation without clearance.

**6. Proof** *(authority and conversion)*
Reviews, testimonials, press features, guest reactions. The couple's words, verbatim.

**7. The team and the process** *(authority)*
How the work happens. Staffing density, the run sheet, the questions asked. This is
where the fear actually breaks.

**8. Lead magnet and offer** *(conversion)*
The guide, the checklist, the cost ranges, the enquiry. One ask, clearly stated.

## Weekly shape

A working week, roughly:

- 3 reels: one aesthetic, one authority, one either
- 2 carousels: one authority, one conversion
- 5 to 7 story sets: mostly aesthetic, one conversion
- 1 proof post: review, feature, or recap

Pinned posts sit outside the calendar and change rarely.
