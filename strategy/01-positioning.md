# Positioning

## The one sentence

BaliVIP plans destination weddings in Bali for couples who are not in Bali, and the
product is not the wedding. It is the removal of fear.

## Who is actually reading

Couples planning from Australia, Singapore, India, Hong Kong, the UK. They already
want a Bali wedding. They already know planners exist. They have not chosen one.

In Eugene Schwartz's terms they are **solution aware moving to product aware**. So do
not sell the idea of Bali and do not explain what a planner is. Both are wasted
slides. Channel the desire they already have and differentiate on proof.

## What they are buying

A third of the reviews open by naming fear. They are wiring a large sum to strangers
in a country most have never visited, for a day that cannot be rescheduled, based on
a website and a WhatsApp thread.

That is the purchase. Not beauty. Everyone has beautiful photographs.

## Audience segments

Write to one of these per post. Never all four at once.

| Segment | What they fear most | What moves them |
|---|---|---|
| **Indian / South Asian family wedding** | Nobody here understands our events | Named rituals, spaces per event, family involvement, food |
| **International / mixed couple** | Two families, two expectations, one day | Blending traditions, guest experience, one evening done properly |
| **Chinese / East Asian** | The parents are the real decision makers | Sangjit, tea pai, a real indoor room, parents planned for |
| **Australian / Western** | We are too far away to control this | Speed of reply, staffing density, wet weather plans, no surprise costs |

Most enquiries are mixed. When unsure, write to the fear, not the culture.

## The four things that break the fear

In the order they appear in the reviews.

1. **Fast, human replies.** Named constantly. Speed is a feature.
2. **Someone who has run this exact thing before.**
3. **Warmth in the first conversation.** Couples say outright this is why they chose.
4. **Willingness to bend.** BaliVIP has beaten culture specialists who knew the
   customs better, because they built the wedding the couple actually imagined.

Point 4 is the most valuable sentence in the whole review corpus. It is worth more
than any award, because it is a client making the argument instead of the brand.

## Where authority comes from now

The elected chairwoman and wedding association credential is removed from everything
at the client's request. Do not reinstate it. Use these instead:

- **The invisible fix.** What went wrong that the couple never found out about.
- **The questions you ask that others do not.** Can the kitchen run a separate prep
  line. Where does the groom's car turn around. Is the tea ceremony room a real room.
- **Bali specifics.** Permits, tides, traffic, rain, curfews, cliff access.
- **Irreversibility.** You get one take at this day.

## What we are not

Not the cheapest. Not the biggest. Not a venue broker. Not a content brand.

The account exists to produce qualified enquiries. A post that gets saved by other
wedding planners and no couples has failed, however beautiful it is.
