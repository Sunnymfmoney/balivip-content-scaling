# Do not ruin the brand

Short list. Every item here has already cost something.

## Claims

**1. Never invent a number.** Not venues, not weddings, not years, not prices, not
guests. If you cannot say where it came from, it does not go on a slide.

**2. Venue and vendor counts are banned outright.** Not reduced, banned. "Sixteen
venues" shipped five times and was wrong. "50+ venues" replaced it and was also
wrong. Every promise attached to a count had to be walked back. The shortlist is the
promise, and the shortlist is real.

**3. No unqualified superiority.** "The best wedding planner in Bali" is unprovable
and reads as insecure. Prove it through mechanism instead.

**4. The chairwoman and association credential is removed** from everything, at the
client's request. Do not reinstate without a written instruction.

## Reviews and real people

**5. A review is verbatim or it is not used.** Never trim to fit. This has already
happened once, and the trim removed the names of the people being praised.

**6. The photograph on a review slide must be that reviewer's own wedding.** Putting
one couple's face against another couple's words is the worst thing in this document.

**7. Ask before a review goes on a paid ad.** These are public reviews, but public is
not the same as consented to advertising.

**8. Never name a couple who has not agreed to be named.**

## Venues and vendors

**9. Never name a venue in copy** without clearance for that specific post. Venue
relationships are commercial and they change.

**10. Never rank venues publicly.** No "top 5", no "better than". You work with all
of them.

**11. Never post a competitor comparison.** The Naresh review does that work for you,
in a client's voice, which is the only acceptable version.

## Craft

**12. No copy on a face.** Ever.

**13. No text on the bright part of a photograph** without a scrim. Check the render,
not the intention.

**14. No em dashes.** Comma, full stop, or rewrite.

**15. No trade jargon.** If the internal team has to ask what a line means, it does
not publish. "Floor and ceiling" and "no discovery call before the discovery call"
both shipped and both had to be pulled.

**16. Never open a video on a static seated portrait,** including the founder's. It
is excellent later for credibility and wrong as a first frame.

## Tone

**17. Do not beg.** No "no pressure", no "we would love to", no asking permission.

**18. Do not use false urgency.** Ever. It is the fastest way to turn a luxury brand
into a discount brand.

**19. Do not chase trends that do not fit.** A trending audio does not override the
brand. Most viral formats are wrong for a purchase this serious.

**20. Do not post the same photograph twice** inside a set that publishes together.

## The test

Before anything goes out, ask: **would a couple spending a large sum on the most
irreversible day of their life feel more confident, or less, after seeing this?**

Less confident means do not post it, however many views it might get.
